From discovery to decision

How to use your JoyaGoo spreadsheet shortlist

Keep the reference traceable

  1. Open the catalog listing.Compare the title and photos with the item you intended to find. Record its catalog ID.
  2. Locate the original seller link.If the listing provides a Weidian, Taobao or other marketplace address, save it separately. Do not use a catalog ID as a marketplace ID.
  3. Check purchasing support.If using JoyaGoo, confirm that it accepts the original link and the selected product. A link opening successfully does not guarantee the agent can purchase it.
  4. Record the exact variant.Keep the size, colour, quantity, current price and seller measurements together before placing an order.

Build a useful comparison

Compare two or three items from the same category. A lower displayed price can refer to a different variant, material or accessory rather than the whole item shown.

For clothing, compare garment measurements with a piece you own. For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and any available length measurements. For bags, confirm dimensions, closure, strap length and what is included.

Keep a copy of the option you selected and the relevant listing details. This makes it easier to compare your order with warehouse photos later.

Read the evidence

Seller photos and QC photos answer different questions

Seller images help identify a design. Historical quality-control photos show a previously photographed item. Neither guarantees what a future order will look like, and images alone cannot establish authenticity, durability or material composition.

Before you buy

JoyaGoo spreadsheet questions

Is this the official JoyaGoo website?

No. This is an independent discovery and buying guide. The two main buttons open HipobuyFind, and product links open the corresponding HipobuyFind listings. Orders, payments and shipping are handled on the services you choose to use.

Is this a downloadable spreadsheet?

This is a browsable web selection with product references and local search tools. It does not claim to offer an official JoyaGoo spreadsheet file or a complete export of the HipobuyFind database.

Are all catalog items available through JoyaGoo?

That has not been verified. Locate the original seller link, check agent purchasing support and confirm the exact variant before paying. An item appearing in this catalog does not establish compatibility with a particular agent.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

Treat them as source-catalog item price references. Do not assume domestic shipping, international freight, service fees or taxes are included. The approximate USD values reproduce the source snapshot and are not live exchange-rate quotes.

What should I do when an old product link fails?

Return to the saved original seller address and check whether the listing is still available. If you have a marketplace product ID, use it with the correct marketplace. Avoid guessing conversion links or replacing an old domain without confirming the destination and item.

Do QC photos guarantee quality?

No. Photos can help assess visible details of the item shown. They do not prove authenticity, comfort, durability or the condition of a future order. Review photos of your actual order when available and check applicable return terms before shipment.
